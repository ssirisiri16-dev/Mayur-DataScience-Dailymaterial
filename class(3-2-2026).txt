Chi-square Test:
-it is a non parametric test(mean,sd)
-we dont required any parameter in this test .
-we are using chi squaure test to check the two categorical columns are having
a realtion  or not
-chi square test dealing with categorical column.

process:

step1:defined H0 and H1
   H0:Both columns are independent
   H1:Both columns are not indepenedent
step2:we need to form a contengency matrix/oberserved matrix .
      it can be given 
	  we need to make 
step3:
     we need to find the expected matraix:
	      (row_sum*column_sum)/total_sum 
step4:
     chi2_value=summation((observed-Expected)**2/expected)
step5: find the degree of freedom
       total no of possibilies
	   dof=(no_of_rows-1)*(no_of_columns-1)
step6:find the critical value for 5% significance level with dof 
     from chi-square table.
step7:
      if chi2_value>critical value--->H0 is rejected 
	                                  H1 is accepted .
ex:
 researcher doing a survey gender and the genre of the movie are having 
 a relation.
 sample_size=100
 H0-->gender and movie genre are independent 
 H1-->gender and movie genere are not independent
